### Non-blocking notes
1. **A notification tapped from another tab does not open its conversation.** Repro:
   - Chat tab with the conversation open, then the Home tab, home gesture, a message from the peer, tap the banner.
   - The app selects the Chat tab, but the page shows the chat *list* (with "026958f0…5ef8 ↓ g3b-route-1"),
     not the conversation, and stays there.
   - From the Chat tab (list or conversation) the same tap opens the conversation.
   - The cause looks like the tab switch itself. Chat with a conversation open → Home → Chat, with no
     notification at all, also comes back on the list. That contradicts `ChatPage`'s comment ("Switching tabs
     keeps it: a reload would drop the open conversation …").
   - The router's `selectRecipient` presumably runs and is then undone by that reset, or runs before it.
   - The gate's step, as written (the conversation already on screen), cannot tell a routed tap from a page
     left where it was. The author's peer test has the same blind spot.
2. **Past the grace, the message was lost, not delivered on reopening** (step 3.4). skychat on the sending side
   wrote it into a connection to the suspended visor. After 20 s without a receipt it dropped the connection
   ("next send will redial"), but it never re-sent the unacknowledged message: not on the link's recovery, not
   on Retry, not on the next send. The sender sees one tick indefinitely. This is skychat's DM delivery, not
   the iOS app. It matters for the Lane D claim that suspended-app delivery "needs no design change": any
   window in which the phone's core is suspended or unreachable loses messages this way.
3. The Chat tab's badge counts messages in the conversation on screen. It showed 1 while "g3b-android-1" was
   being read, 2 after the image, and 1 after the tapped "g3b-bg-1". `/unread` does not drop while the page
   shows the thread, at least not within the 10 s poll.
4. `background grace began (unbounded)`: the remaining time is read straight after `beginBackgroundTask`, while
   UIKit still reports no limit. The grace actually expired 27.5 s later. The log line never shows the real
   figure.
5. The Skynet link between the two visors worked for the first exchange and then failed ("dial … context
   deadline exceeded", 04:37:13). DMSG and Retry delivered, as "M3 as built" describes. After the page's reset
   in note 1, the reopened conversation's selector was back on Skynet. The network choice is not kept per
   conversation, which weakens the "pick DMSG first" advice.
6. The permission fix is visible: on this fresh device the stream opened with permission 2 fourteen seconds
   after Connect, and the prompt came at once. It no longer waits on the prompt.
7. Bench: the emulator's skychat is not a fresh identity (026958f0…5ef8, as in the first review). Its config is
   stamped v1.3.96-105-g83f6a7769, and the v0.0.0-3de84bb09 core accepts it by design: an unstamped core skips
   the version check (`visorconfig/parse.go`). This fits finding (5) (a tagged core refuses an untagged config),
   which remains open.
