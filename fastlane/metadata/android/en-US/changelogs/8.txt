SkyMail has its own contact list, deletes many messages at once, sends every address over the network you pick, and no longer fails or doubles a large send. The wallet shows each address's balance, sends from one address, lets Skycoin sends pick their coins and hours, tries a list of nodes per coin, and restores older 24-word seeds. The app is now in French, and the whole chat page is translated. Deleting a chat or contact ends its message approval.
