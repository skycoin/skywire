Skywire is a decentralized network run by its users. This app runs a full Skywire node (a "visor") on your phone and gives you the apps that use it:

<ul>
<li><b>SkyChat</b>: end-to-end encrypted messages, groups, voice calls and file sharing, carried peer to peer over Skywire with no central chat server.</li>
<li><b>SkyVPN</b>: route the whole phone's traffic through a Skywire VPN server, with an optional killswitch.</li>
<li><b>SkySOCKS</b>: a local SOCKS5 proxy through a public Skywire proxy server, for apps that support one.</li>
<li><b>Wallet</b>: a Skycoin (SKY) wallet whose keys never leave the phone, with QR send and receive.</li>
<li><b>SkyDEX</b>: trade on the Skywire decentralized exchange.</li>
</ul>

Your node has its own cryptographic identity. You can export or replace it, lock the app behind the phone's screen lock or fingerprint, and grant a desktop Skywire hypervisor remote management of the phone.

The node runs as a foreground service so connections survive in the background. Skywire uses Skycoin's public discovery servers to find other nodes; their software is open source in the same repository.
